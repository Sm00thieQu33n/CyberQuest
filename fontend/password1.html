Password Security 1

You are approached by a coworker who is having password issues. They ask you "Hey, I'm locked out of my account and need to finish something really quickly. Can you give me your password so I can log in from your computer?"

What should you do here?

Responses:

  • A. Give them your password because you trust your coworker.
  • B. Give them your password, but change it afterward.
  • C. Refuse to share your password and direct them to the appropriate IT/account recovery process.
  • D. Log into your account for them and let them use it.
