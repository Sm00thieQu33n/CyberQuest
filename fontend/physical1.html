Physical Security 1

Say you are working in an office or library and you notice a coworker's computer sitting unattended and unlocked. On the screen is sensitive-looking information. A person you don't recognize walks over to the desk and starts looking at the screen.

What should you do here?

Choices:

  • A. Ignore it because it's not your computer.
  • B. Take a picture of the screen so you can report it later.
  • C. Alert the coworker or appropriate staff/security person about the situation.
  • D. Close the computer's applications and leave.
